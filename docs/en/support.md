---
title: Support
---

- **Bug in the API or in this documentation**: [open an issue](https://github.com/velesan/developer.globkurier.pl/issues/new/choose).
  Give the endpoint, environment (production / test), time of the request and the response you got.
- **Security vulnerability**: do not open a public issue, follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/velesan/developer.globkurier.pl/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

!!! warning
    Issues are public. Remove tokens, passwords and personal data of recipients before posting.
